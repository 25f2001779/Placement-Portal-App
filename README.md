The Project is about Placement Portal App 
Admin:
The Admin is the central authority who oversees the entire system and ensures it runs smoothly. They use a dashboard to monitor total count of students, participating companies, and active placement drives. As the primary gatekeeper, the Admin is responsible for reviewing every company registration and job posting, with the power to approve or reject them. They have full control over the database, allowing them to search for specific students or companies. If any user violates the rules, the Admin can deactivate their account, or permanently blacklist them from the portal. Additionally, they manage all application records, viewing both upcoming and past placement history. Ultimately, the Admin ensures that the platform remains a secure and organized environment for both hiring and seeking jobs.
Company:
Companies serve as the recruiters on the platform and must begin by registering a profile to showcase their details. They are granted access to the system's main features only after the Admin verifies and approves their account. Once active, companies can create and manage placement drives by posting job roles that include specific requirements like skills, experience, and salary ranges. They use a dashboard to track their posted jobs and see exactly how many students have applied to each one. This allows them to review individual student profiles and download resumes to find candidate. Throughout the hiring process, the company is responsible for updating application statuses, moving candidates from "Shortlisted" to "Selected" or "Rejected." They can also edit their job postings or close them once the hiring goal is met, ensuring their dashboard stays current.
Student:
Students use the platform as a personal career hub where they can self-register and maintain a professional digital presence. After logging in, they can update their profiles with their education and skills and must upload a resume to be considered for roles. Their dashboard provides a clear view of all placement drives that are approved by the Admin, making it easy to find opportunities. Students can search for specific jobs and apply for them directly through the portal. Once an application is submitted, they can track its status in real-time and view their entire placement history in one place. This process allows students to focus on their applications while staying organized and updated on their job search progress.
